Chart widget: SEC Form D private placement filings

A free embeddable chart. Static script and static JSON: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking. It reads /api/sec-form-d-funding-leads.json and draws an SVG bar chart.

Embed (script tag)

<script src="https://mamoo85.github.io/open-data-actors/widgets/oda-chart.js" async></script>
<oda-chart dataset="sec-form-d-funding-leads"></oda-chart>

Embed (iframe)

<iframe src="https://mamoo85.github.io/open-data-actors/widgets/embed/sec-form-d-funding-leads.html" width="100%" height="380" style="border:0" loading="lazy" title="SEC Form D private placement filings"></iframe>

Options

Attributes on <oda-chart>: group (column to group by), metric (count or sum:column), top (bars, default 10), title. Example: <oda-chart dataset="sec-form-d-funding-leads" group="issuerState" metric="count">.
