Nursing Home Ratings, Fines & Penalties

How it works

Get US nursing home star ratings, fines, payment denials, abuse flags and special focus status from the free CMS Provider Data Catalog, one row per facility with phone, address, beds and a 0-100 risk score. No login, no proxy. Filter by state, city, star rating, ownership and fines.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
statesStatesnoTwo-letter US state codes, for example MI, OH, TX. Leave empty for all states (about 14,700 facilities).
cityCity containsnoOptional. Keep facilities whose city contains this text, for example "Detroit".
minOverallRatingMinimum overall star ratingnoOptional 1 to 5. Keep facilities rated at or above this.
maxOverallRatingMaximum overall star ratingnoOptional 1 to 5. Set to 2 to find low-rated facilities. Facilities with no rating are dropped when either rating limit is set.
onlyWithFinesOnly facilities with finesnoTurn on to keep only facilities with at least one fine or a fine total above zero in the last three years.
ownershipTypeOwnership type containsnoOptional. Text such as "For profit", "Non profit" or "Government".
sortBySort bynoOrder of the rows. Rows are cut to Max rows after sorting.
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many rows. You pay only for rows delivered. Hard cap 20,000.

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
