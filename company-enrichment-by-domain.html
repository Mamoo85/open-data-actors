Turn a list of domains into company profiles

Who it is for: Sales ops teams cleaning a CRM list of bare domains.

The problem

You have domains but not names, socials, careers pages or contact channels.

How it works

Paste domains; get company name, description, socials, careers URL, ATS and email provider, and contact details found on the site.
