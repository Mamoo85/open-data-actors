Free Data Tools

Free in-browser tools, a company lookup, cost calculators and a data source quiz. No sign-up, no tracking, no API keys.

  • App Store Review Summary
    Enter an iPhone app ID and get its rating, the latest reviews, the rating split and the most common complaint words from Apple's public feed.
  • Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby Job Board Viewer
    Enter a company board name and list its open jobs from the public Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby job board API, then filter and export to CSV.
  • Check a Company: Free Open-Source Lookup
    Look up a company in GLEIF, Wikidata, USAspending, job boards, Hacker News and DNS from your browser, with a clear list of what it cannot check.
  • Cost Calculator: Funding, grants and compliance
    Estimate the monthly pay-per-event cost of the funding, grants and compliance actors from their live published prices.
  • Cost Calculator: Hiring and jobs
    Estimate the monthly pay-per-event cost of the hiring and jobs actors from their live published prices.
  • Cost Calculator: Monitoring and research
    Estimate the monthly pay-per-event cost of the monitoring and research actors from their live published prices.
  • Cost Calculator: Sales and lead generation
    Estimate the monthly pay-per-event cost of the sales and lead generation actors from their live published prices.
  • Cost Calculator: Websites, SEO and tech
    Estimate the monthly pay-per-event cost of the websites, seo and tech actors from their live published prices.
  • Pay-per-Event Cost Calculator
    What do these Apify actors cost? Prices read from each actor's published pay-per-event pricing (snapshot 2026-10-13).
  • Domain DNS and Email Setup Checker
    Look up a domain's A, MX, NS, SPF and DMARC records and its registration age, using free public DNS and RDAP services.
  • Email Extractor and MX Check
    Paste any text and pull out the email addresses, de-duplicated, with role inboxes flagged and an optional check that each domain can receive mail.
  • Federal Awards by Recipient
    Search US federal contracts or grants awarded to a company or organisation, using the free USAspending.gov API directly from your browser.
  • On-Page SEO Audit from HTML
    Paste a page's HTML source and check title, description, headings, canonical, images, social tags and structured data in your browser.
  • Sanctions Name Match Explainer
    See how two names compare after transliteration, word-order and honorific normalisation, the way screening tools do.
  • Sitemap XML Analyzer
    Paste a sitemap.xml and get URL counts by section, duplicates, stale lastmod dates and format problems, all in your browser.
  • USDOT and MC Number Checker
    Clean, validate and de-duplicate trucking USDOT and MC numbers, with one-click links to the official FMCSA records.
  • Website Tech Stack Hint Checker
    Paste a page source and see which CMS, frameworks, analytics and widgets it uses, detected in your browser.
  • Which Data Source Do I Need?
    Answer four questions and get a recommendation for which of our public-data actors fits, a rough cost, and a free option where one exists.

Disclosure: we build and maintain the Apify actors linked here. This page is free, has no tracking or analytics, and does not store anything; inputs stay in your browser except where the page says it calls a named public API.
