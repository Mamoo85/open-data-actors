Check a Company: Free Open-Source Lookup

Look up a company in GLEIF, Wikidata, USAspending, job boards, Hacker News and DNS from your browser, with a clear list of what it cannot check.

Looks a company up across free, key-free public sources, directly from your browser: GLEIF (legal entity registry), Wikidata, USAspending.gov, public job board APIs, Hacker News search, and (if you give a domain) public DNS and RDAP. It is a quick first look, not due diligence. The section at the bottom lists what it cannot do.

Disclosure: we build and maintain the Apify actors linked here. This page is free, has no tracking or analytics, and does not store anything; inputs stay in your browser except where the page says it calls a named public API.
