Federal Awards by Recipient

Search US federal contracts or grants awarded to a company or organisation, using the free USAspending.gov API directly from your browser.

Calls the public USAspending.gov API (no key needed) straight from your browser; nothing goes through our servers. Name matching is fuzzy and a company can appear under several legal names or subsidiaries, so check the recipient column. Shows the 25 largest awards that had activity in the period (the amount shown is the award's total, not just the period); it is not a complete audit and USAspending data can lag the source agencies.
