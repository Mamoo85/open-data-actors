Domain DNS and Email Setup Checker

Look up a domain's A, MX, NS, SPF and DMARC records and its registration age, using free public DNS and RDAP services.

Calls two free public services directly from your browser: Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS (records) and the RDAP bootstrap at rdap.org (registration date and registrar). Nothing goes through our servers. RDAP data for some country domains is limited or blocked, and DNS cannot tell you what is on the website itself.
