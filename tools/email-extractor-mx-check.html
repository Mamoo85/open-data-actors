Email Extractor and MX Check

Paste any text and pull out the email addresses, de-duplicated, with role inboxes flagged and an optional check that each domain can receive mail.

Everything is parsed in your browser. The optional MX check sends only the unique domain names (not the addresses) to Cloudflare's public DNS-over-HTTPS service. An MX record means the domain accepts mail; it cannot prove that a specific mailbox exists. Handles "name [at] domain [dot] com" style obfuscation.
