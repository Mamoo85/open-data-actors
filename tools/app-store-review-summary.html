App Store Review Summary

Enter an iPhone app ID and get its rating, the latest reviews, the rating split and the most common complaint words from Apple's public feed.

Uses Apple's public iTunes lookup and customer-review RSS feeds, called from your browser (no key). The review feed returns only the most recent reviews per country (this page reads up to 250), not the full history, and Apple can change or remove the feed. App ID is the number in the App Store URL after "id", e.g. apps.apple.com/us/app/spotify/id324684580.
