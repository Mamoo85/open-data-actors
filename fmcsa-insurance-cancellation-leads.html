FMCSA Insurance Cancellation Leads for Agents

How it works

Find motor carriers whose FMCSA-filed liability or cargo insurance is scheduled to cancel. Each lead has the insurer, policy, cancellation date, a flag for replacement coverage already on file, business phone and email, address, fleet size and a 0-100 lead score. Public FMCSA data, no login.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
daysAheadDays aheadnoKeep filings whose cancellation effective date falls within the next N days (0 = today only). Default 30.
daysBackDays backnoAlso keep filings that already cancelled in the last N days (carriers that may be uninsured right now). Default 0.
insuranceTypesCoverage typesnoWhich filed coverage types to include. Liability (BIPD) is the one most carriers must keep on file to operate.
statesCarrier statesnoOptional two-letter US state codes of the carrier physical address, for example TX, CA. Leave empty for all states.
minPowerUnitsMinimum power unitsnoKeep carriers reporting at least this many trucks and tractors on their latest MCS-150 census form.
excludeWithReplacementExclude carriers with replacement coveragenoOn by default. Drops filings where the carrier already has a different active or pending policy of the same coverage type on file.
activeCarriersOnlyActive carriers onlynoOn by default. Drops carriers whose USDOT status in the census file is not active.
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many rows, highest lead score first. You pay only for rows delivered. Hard cap 20,000.
