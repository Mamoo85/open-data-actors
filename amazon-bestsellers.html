Amazon Best Sellers top 100 as a table, 21 countries

Who it is for: Amazon sellers and brands watching their categories, product researchers and dropshippers, affiliate and review sites, retail analysts, AI agents.

The problem

Amazon publishes its top 100 per category for five lists in every country, but only as web pages, 50 at a time, with ranks 31-50 loaded by script.

How it works

Pick a country (21 Amazon sites), list types and categories by name or URL. Each run reads every rank Amazon shows (1-100) per list, optionally every subcategory, and writes one row per product with rank, ASIN, title, price and currency, stars, review count, full-size image and the category path. Filters for rating, price and reviews; optional product-page details (brand, bullets, seller, availability, all Best Sellers Ranks); monitor mode outputs only products that are new or moved since the last run.

Limits, stated plainly

At most 100 ranks per list, as Amazon publishes. Movers & Shakers, Most Wished For and Gift Ideas are often empty on Amazon itself; empty lists are reported and cost nothing. List prices are the lowest current offer on the card. Public pages only, no login, no personal data.

Price

Pay per result on Apify, no subscription: $0.0019 per ranked product on the free plan, $0.0015 Bronze, $0.0012 Silver, $0.001 Gold and above, plus $0.00005 per run start. Empty lists, filtered rows and unchanged rows in monitor mode are not charged. The live price is on the actor page.
