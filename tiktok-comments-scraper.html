TikTok comments scraper: comments, likes and replies as a table

Who it is for: Brand and community teams reading audience feedback, researchers, sentiment and NLP teams and AI agents.

The problem

TikTok shows comments in a slow in-app scroll with replies behind taps. Getting the top 1,000 comments of a video into a spreadsheet is slow by hand.

How it works

Give public video URLs. Each run returns one row per comment with text, likes, reply count, date, language, pinned flag and a link. Replies can be added as rows. The free sample leaves out commenter names.
