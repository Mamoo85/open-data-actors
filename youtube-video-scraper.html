YouTube video scraper: views, likes, dates and tags as a table

Who it is for: Creators, agencies, researchers, SEO teams and AI agents that need YouTube video data in a spreadsheet.

The problem

YouTube shows one video or one search page at a time. Collecting views, likes, exact dates, tags and chapters for 300 videos on a topic by hand takes hours.

How it works

Give search terms, channel or playlist URLs, hashtags or video URLs. Each run returns one row per video: title, channel and subscribers, views, likes, comment count, exact date, duration, description, tags, chapters and subtitle languages.
