Google Trends API: City & Metro Interest, Related, Trending

How it works

Google Trends down to city and metro level, without a browser: interest over time, by region, city and metro, related queries and topics (top and rising), compare up to 5 keywords, and Trending now with search volume and news. Clean JSON rows, any country, any period.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
modeWhat to scrapenointerest = one row per keyword. compare = keywords charted against each other (groups of 5). trendingNow = what people search for right now. suggest = Google's topic suggestions...
searchTermsKeywordsnoSearch terms or topics, for example bitcoin or best running shoes. In compare mode they are grouped 5 at a time and measured on one shared 0-100 scale.
startUrlsGoogle Trends URLsnoOptional. Paste trends.google.com/trends/explore links; keywords, region, period, category and search type are read from the link.
timeRangeTime rangenoHourly data for the last hour or 4 hours, daily for 7 days, weekly for a year, monthly for 5 years.
customTimeRangeCustom time rangenoOverrides the time range. 2024-01-01 2024-06-30 (start end) or 2024-01-01 (start until today).
geoCountry or regionnoEmpty = worldwide. ISO country code (US, GB, DE), a region (US-CA, GB-ENG) or a US metro (US-CA-807). In Trending now, empty = US.
geosMore countries or regionsnoRun every keyword once per extra region as well. One row per keyword and region.
categoryGoogle category idno0 = all categories. Examples: 3 Arts and Entertainment, 5 Computers and Electronics, 7 Finance, 12 Business and Industrial, 22 Books and Literature, 44 Beauty and Fitness, 47...
propertySearch typenoWhich Google search the interest is measured on.
includeTimelineInterest over timenoThe time series (0-100). The summary (average, peak, latest, trend) is always included.
includeRegionsInterest by regionnoCountries when worldwide, states or regions inside a country.
includeCitiesInterest by citynoCities with latitude and longitude. Adds one request per keyword.
includeMetrosInterest by US metronoUS media markets (DMA). Empty outside the US.
includeRelatedQueriesRelated queriesnoTop and rising related searches.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
