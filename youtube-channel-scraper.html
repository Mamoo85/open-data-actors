YouTube channel scraper: subscribers, totals and latest videos

Who it is for: Creator-economy teams, agencies vetting channels, sponsorship researchers and AI agents.

The problem

A channel page shows subscribers and totals but gives no export. Comparing 50 channels, with country, join date, links and recent uploads, means opening each by hand.

How it works

Give @handles, channel URLs or IDs. Each run returns a channel row (subscribers, total views, video count, country, joined date, links, avatar and banner) plus newest, popular or oldest videos, Shorts and streams if you ask for them.
