USDA Organic Certified Operations: New Certification Leads

How it works

Find newly certified and recently updated USDA organic businesses from the public Organic INTEGRITY Database: operation, certifier, scopes, products, status date, city, state and a new-registration flag. No login, no personal data.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
sinceDaysDays since status changenoKeep operations whose certification status took effect within this many days. 90 shows the newest certifications. Use 0 for no date limit.
certificationStatusCertification statusnoStatus of the operation. Certified is the usual choice for lead lists.
statesStatesnoOptional US state codes or names, for example MI, OH, California. Empty keeps every state and country.
countriesCountriesnoOptional country names, for example United States, Mexico, Canada.
scopesCertified scopesnoOptional. Keep operations certified in at least one of these scopes. HANDLING covers processors, packers and distributors.
productContainsProducts containnoOptional text that must appear in the certified products, for example blueberries, eggs or maple.
nameContainsOperation name containsnoOptional text that must appear in the operation name or former names.
certifierContainsCertifier containsnoOptional text that must appear in the certifying agent name, for example Oregon Tilth.
onlyNewToRegistryOnly new to the registrynoKeep only operations that were not in the previous snapshot, which means first-time listings rather than renewals or reinstatements.
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many rows, newest status change first.

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
