One day of SEC Form D filings: 50 filings, median offering $2,498,312

Form D is the notice a company files with the SEC when it raises money without a public offering. We looked at 50 filings dated 2026-10-02.

Size

44 filings state a total offering amount. The median is $2,498,312, the middle half runs from $1,075,000 to $9,250,000, and the range is $700 to $351,903,103. 6 filings describe the offering as indefinite (no fixed amount).

How much was sold

For the 44 filings with both a total offering and an amount sold, the sold total is 72.2% of the offered total. 10 report nothing sold yet and 13 report the offering fully sold. The median number of investors is 2.5 (largest: 32).

Who files

Industry groups: Other (13), Other Technology (11), Other Real Estate (5), Biotechnology (4). Most common states: CA (5), NY (5), CO (4), TX (3). Entity types: Corporation (24), Limited Liability Company (20), Limited Partnership (4). 34 of 50 filers chose "Decline to Disclose" for revenue range. Where the year of incorporation is given (36 filings), 26 were incorporated in 2025 or 2026. 5 filings report sales commissions or finders fees.

Limits

  • One filing date. Filings cluster around deadlines and weekdays, so another day will look different.
  • The "Other" industry group is the largest, which tells you little about what these companies do.
  • Amounts are as reported by the filer. We did not check them against anything else.
  • The ratio of sold to offered is dominated by a few large filings.

Data and method

Input: 50 filings from a test run of the sec-form-d-funding-leads actor, stored as secd_filings.json with phone, street address and named-person fields removed (the public sample CSV has 12 rows). The figures on this page are computed by a2_form_d.py (run it from the articles/_data folder). Related: the actor page and its sample CSV. Published 2026-10-13. We build and maintain these actors.
