SEC 13F Institutional Holdings Tracker

How it works

Tracker for institutional holdings from free SEC EDGAR 13F-HR filings: one row per position with issuer, CUSIP, value, shares, put/call, portfolio weight, quarter, filing date, accession link and quarter-over-quarter share change. Institutions only, no login, no personal data.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
managersManagersnoSEC CIK numbers (digits) or exact manager names. Default: Berkshire Hathaway, Pershing Square, Bridgewater, Renaissance Technologies and Tiger Global.
topHoldingsPerManagerTop holdings per managernoLargest positions by reported value kept for each manager.
includeQuarterChangeCompare with previous quarternoAdds previous shares, share change and new/increased/decreased/unchanged from the prior 13F-HR filing.
minValueUsdMinimum position value (USD)noOptional. Skip positions below this reported value.
issuerContainsIssuer name containsnoOptional text that must appear in the issuer name, for example "apple" or "energy".
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many rows.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
