Amazon product search results as a clean table

Who it is for: Sellers and brands tracking competitors, price monitors, affiliate and comparison sites, market researchers.

The problem

Amazon search and product pages are hard to turn into a table of prices, ratings and ASINs.

How it works

Give keywords, search URLs, product URLs or ASINs and pick a marketplace (US, UK, DE or CA); get one row per unique product with price, list price, rating, review count, Prime flag, badges, bought-in-past-month, breadcrumbs, images, features, dimensions and weight.

Limits, stated plainly

Public pages only, with no login and no cookies. No customer reviews, questions or reviewer data. Amazon may block some IPs; blocked pages are retried on a fresh session and are not charged. Prime and delivery info can vary by location. The US marketplace is the most tested.

Price

Pay per result on Apify, no subscription: $0.004 per product on the free plan, falling to $0.0026 on Gold and above, plus $0.00005 per run start. Duplicates and blocked pages are not charged. The live price is on the actor page.
