Apollo Alternative for Local Businesses: Verified Emails

How it works

Get verified emails for local businesses for outreach, including ones with no website of their own. Each email is MX-checked and comes with its source URL and a confidence score. Pay only for verified emails.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
