YouTube Shorts scraper: views, likes and dates as a table

Who it is for: Creators, agencies, researchers and AI agents that need YouTube Shorts data in a spreadsheet.

The problem

YouTube shows Shorts one at a time. Collecting views, likes, comment counts and exact publish dates for the Shorts of a channel by hand takes hours.

How it works

Give channel URLs or single Shorts URLs. Each run returns one row per Short: title, views, likes, comment count, exact publish date, duration, hashtags, description and channel stats. Sort by newest, popular or oldest and filter by date.
