Web design leads: local businesses with no or weak websites

Who it is for: Web designers, freelancers and agencies who want local businesses that actually need a new site.

The problem

Finding businesses with no site, a broken site or a dated one means opening hundreds of Maps listings and their websites by hand.

How it works

Give a business type and a city. The actor finds businesses on Google Maps, checks the website (missing, broken, no HTTPS, not mobile-friendly, outdated, parked, Facebook-only) and scores each lead 0-100 with plain-English reasons. Phone columns are left out of the sample CSV.
