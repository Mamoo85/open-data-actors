Instagram posts scraper: captions, likes, comments and carousels

Who it is for: Brand and agency teams, researchers comparing content, and AI agents.

The problem

Instagram shows posts one at a time and asks for a login after a few views. Pulling captions, likes and comments for hundreds of posts by hand is not practical.

How it works

Give public profile or post URLs. Each run returns one row per post: type, caption, hashtags, mentions, likes, comment counts, first and latest comments, media and carousel items, views or plays for video, and timestamp.
