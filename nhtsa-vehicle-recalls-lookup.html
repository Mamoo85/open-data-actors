NHTSA Vehicle Recalls Lookup: Car Recalls by Model, Year

How it works

NHTSA vehicle recalls lookup: car recalls and other US vehicle safety recalls by make, model and year from the free NHTSA API. Campaign number, component, summary, consequence, remedy, park-it flags and a severity score. No login, no VINs, no personal data.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
vehiclesVehicles (Make,Model,Year)noOne vehicle per line as Make,Model,Year, for example Toyota,Camry,2020. Leave the model out (Honda,2019) to cover every model of that make and year. Leave the list empty to use...
parkWarningsOnlyPark-it or park-outside onlynoTurn on to keep only recalls where NHTSA says owners should stop driving or park outside.
minSeverityScoreMinimum severity scorenoOptional 0 to 100. Keep only rows at or above this score.
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many rows, highest severity first. You pay only for rows delivered.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
