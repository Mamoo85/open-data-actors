TikTok hashtag scraper: videos by hashtag or search with engagement stats

Who it is for: Social media and trend teams, brand researchers, creators looking for what works in a niche and AI agents.

The problem

TikTok search shows a few videos at a time with no numbers side by side. Finding the top videos for a hashtag, with views and engagement, means opening each one.

How it works

Give hashtags or search phrases. Each run returns one row per video with text, link, date, views, likes, comments, shares, saves, engagement rate, hashtags, music and author stats. Creator handles are in the full output but not in the sample.

Limits, stated plainly

Public videos only. TikTok decides which videos a hashtag page lists, so a run returns what is publicly listed, not every video with the tag.
