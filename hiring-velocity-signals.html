Hiring velocity signals: company growth from job posts

Who it is for: Sales teams timing outreach, recruiters, investors and analysts watching which companies are hiring faster.

The problem

A company's careers page shows open roles today but not whether hiring is speeding up. Judging growth across 100 companies by hand is not practical.

How it works

Give company names. Each run finds the careers source and returns open roles, new roles in the last 7 and 30 days, a weekly velocity, a trend against the previous window and a hiring score. Counts are flagged when they are only a lower bound.
