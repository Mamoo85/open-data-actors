OpenStreetMap Business Leads: Find No-Website Leads

How it works

Local business leads from OpenStreetMap by city, county, bounding box or radius: name, category, address, phone, website, business email, opening hours and socials, with a no-website flag, completeness score and last-edit date. Keyless, new-only monitor mode.
