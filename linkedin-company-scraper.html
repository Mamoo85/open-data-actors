LinkedIn company scraper: size, industry, followers and headquarters, no login

Who it is for: Sales ops, lead enrichment, recruiters, market researchers and AI agents that need company facts keyed by a domain or LinkedIn page.

The problem

A list of 500 company names has no sizes or industries. Looking each up on LinkedIn, one tab at a time, takes days and breaks as soon as you are logged out.

How it works

Give LinkedIn company URLs, slugs, website domains or plain names. Each run returns one row per company: name, industry, size band, employees on LinkedIn, followers, headquarters, website, specialties and locations.

Limits, stated plainly

Public company pages only. Fields LinkedIn leaves blank for small companies stay null. Name-only input is matched by website and can pick the wrong page for very common names; use a domain when you can.
