SEC Company Financials from XBRL (10-K, 10-Q)

How it works

Get annual or quarterly company financials from free SEC XBRL filings: revenue, net income, assets, liabilities, cash flow, free cash flow, margins, growth and a 0-100 fundamentals score per company and fiscal period. Enter tickers or CIKs. No key, no login, no proxy.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
tickersStock tickersnoUS-listed stock tickers, for example AAPL or MSFT. Up to 200 companies per run.
ciksSEC CIK numbers (optional)noOptional SEC Central Index Key numbers, with or without leading zeros, for example 320193. Useful for companies without a ticker.
periodTypePeriod typenoAnnual returns one row per fiscal year from 10-K filings. Quarterly returns one row per quarter from 10-Q filings (Q1 to Q3; the fourth quarter is only in the 10-K and is not...
yearsBackYears backnoHow many fiscal years of history per company, newest first. Quarterly mode returns up to 3 quarters per year.
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many company-period rows. You pay only for rows delivered.
