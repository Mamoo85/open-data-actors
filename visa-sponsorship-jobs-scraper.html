Visa Sponsorship Jobs Scraper for Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby

How it works

Find jobs whose posting text mentions visa sponsorship. Reads public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job boards that you list, flags each posting as sponsors, no_sponsorship, mixed or unknown, and returns the matching sentence as evidence. No login, no proxy.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
