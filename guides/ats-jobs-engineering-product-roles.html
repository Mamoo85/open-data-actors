How to get this yourself

  1. Open the Open jobs from Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby career pages actor on Apify.
  2. Give company slugs; get normalized jobs with location, department, remote flag, posting date and apply link.
  3. Run it, then export the dataset as CSV, JSON or Excel from the results tab.

Limits, stated plainly

You supply the company slug; it does not discover every company. This page shows a small sample, so counts here say nothing about the whole dataset.
