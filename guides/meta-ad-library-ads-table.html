How to get this yourself

  1. Open the Facebook and Instagram ads from the Meta Ad Library actor on Apify.
  2. Give search terms, advertiser Page ids or pasted Ad Library URLs, plus country, active status and media filters; get one flat row per ad with advertiser, ad text, headline, link, call to action, image and video URLs, dates, platforms and variation count.
  3. Run it, then export the dataset as CSV, JSON or Excel from the results tab.

Limits, stated plainly

Returns only what the Ad Library publishes: no targeting, no exact spend for commercial ads and no ad performance. Spend and reach ranges appear only on political and issue ads. Meta rate-limits datacenter IPs, so residential proxy is the default. Image and video URLs are Meta CDN links that expire after some days. This page shows a small sample, so counts here say nothing about the whole dataset.
