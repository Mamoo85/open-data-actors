How to get this yourself

  1. Open the Apple App Store reviews and ratings by country actor on Apify.
  2. Give app IDs or URLs and countries; get app details and reviews with rating, version and date.
  3. Run it, then export the dataset as CSV, JSON or Excel from the results tab.

Limits, stated plainly

Public review feeds only, and reviewer names are not exported in these samples. This page shows a small sample, so counts here say nothing about the whole dataset.
