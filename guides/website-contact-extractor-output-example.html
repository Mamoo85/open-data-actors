How to get this yourself

  1. Open the Emails and phones from a list of company websites actor on Apify.
  2. Paste URLs; get emails, phones and socials found on contact-like pages and the page they came from.
  3. Run it, then export the dataset as CSV, JSON or Excel from the results tab.

Limits, stated plainly

Finds only what is published; some sites list none. Check opt-out and local law before outreach. This page shows a small sample, so counts here say nothing about the whole dataset.
