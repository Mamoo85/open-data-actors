How to get this yourself

  1. Open the Turn a list of domains into company profiles actor on Apify.
  2. Paste domains; get company name, description, socials, careers URL, ATS and email provider, and contact details found on the site.
  3. Run it, then export the dataset as CSV, JSON or Excel from the results tab.

Limits, stated plainly

Only public site data; no private databases, so small sites return less. This page shows a small sample, so counts here say nothing about the whole dataset.
