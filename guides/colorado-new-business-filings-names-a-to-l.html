How to get this yourself

  1. Open the New LLCs and corporations by state actor on Apify.
  2. Select states and days back; get entity name, type, filing date, status and address from open registry data.
  3. Run it, then export the dataset as CSV, JSON or Excel from the results tab.

Limits, stated plainly

Only states with open data are supported; availability varies by state. This page shows a small sample, so counts here say nothing about the whole dataset.
