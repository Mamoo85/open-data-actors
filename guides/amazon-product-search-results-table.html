How to get this yourself

  1. Open the Amazon product search results as a clean table actor on Apify.
  2. Give keywords, search URLs, product URLs or ASINs and pick a marketplace (US, UK, DE or CA); get one row per unique product with price, list price, rating, review count, Prime flag, badges, bought-in-past-month, breadcrumbs, images, features, dimensions and weight.
  3. Run it, then export the dataset as CSV, JSON or Excel from the results tab.

Limits, stated plainly

Public pages only, with no login and no cookies. No customer reviews, questions or reviewer data. Amazon may block some IPs; blocked pages are retried on a fresh session and are not charged. Prime and delivery info can vary by location. The US marketplace is the most tested. This page shows a small sample, so counts here say nothing about the whole dataset.
