How to get this yourself

  1. Open the Local businesses with no website, from OpenStreetMap actor on Apify.
  2. Pick categories and a city; filter to businesses with no website listed.
  3. Run it, then export the dataset as CSV, JSON or Excel from the results tab.

Limits, stated plainly

OSM coverage is uneven, and "no website" can mean the data is outdated. This page shows a small sample, so counts here say nothing about the whole dataset.
