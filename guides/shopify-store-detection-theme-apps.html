How to get this yourself

  1. Open the Shopify store details: theme, apps, products actor on Apify.
  2. Give store domains; get whether it is Shopify, theme, detected apps, product counts and public contact info.
  3. Run it, then export the dataset as CSV, JSON or Excel from the results tab.

Limits, stated plainly

Detection is from public storefront code; hidden apps are not seen. This page shows a small sample, so counts here say nothing about the whole dataset.
