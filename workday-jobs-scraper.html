Jobs from any Workday career site

Who it is for: Job-data teams, recruiters and market analysts.

The problem

Workday sites share a platform but each has its own tenant URL.

How it works

Give company names or career URLs and optional keywords; get title, location, posted date, requisition ID and pay range when present.
