New trucking companies, the week they get authority

Who it is for: Truck insurance agents, ELD/fuel-card/factoring sellers, equipment lenders.

The problem

New carriers are the easiest buyers to win and the hardest to find: they appear in the FMCSA census before they appear on any lead list.

How it works

Pick a state and days-back window; get new carriers with fleet size, driver count, operation type and, where FMCSA lists them, phone and email.
