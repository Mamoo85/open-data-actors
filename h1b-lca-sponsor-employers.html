H-1B Sponsor Employers: LCA Filings, Wages, Titles

How it works

Rank US employers by H-1B sponsorship from the free Department of Labor LCA disclosure data: filings, certifications, top job titles, median offered wage vs prevailing wage, worksites and a sponsor score. Company level only, no login.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
employerNameContainsEmployer name containsnoOptional text that must appear in the employer name, for example "google" or "consulting".
employerStatesEmployer statesnoOptional two-letter state codes of the employer's headquarters address, for example MI, TX. Leave empty for all states.
worksiteStatesWorksite statesnoOptional two-letter state codes. Keep only filings whose worksite is in these states.
jobTitleKeywordJob title keywordnoOptional word in the job title or occupation, for example "data scientist". Counts and wages then cover only matching filings.
minFilingsMinimum filingsnoKeep employers with at least this many filings in the quarter.
minSponsorScoreMinimum sponsor scorenoOptional 0 to 100. Keep only employers at or above this score.
fiscalYearFiscal yearnoOptional federal fiscal year, for example 2025. Leave empty with the quarter for the newest available file.
quarterQuarternoOptional fiscal quarter 1 to 4. Leave empty for the newest file of the chosen year.
sourceUrlSource file URLnoOptional advanced setting: a direct https link to a DOL LCA disclosure .xlsx file.
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many employers, highest sponsor score first.
