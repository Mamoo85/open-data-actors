TikTok profile scraper: followers, posts and engagement

Who it is for: Influencer and brand teams vetting creators, agencies monitoring competitor accounts, social analysts and AI agents.

The problem

TikTok shows reach one video at a time. Comparing a creator set, or checking what a competitor posted this week, means opening every profile by hand.

How it works

Give public usernames or profile URLs. Each run returns the latest posts with views, likes, comments, shares, saves, hashtags, mentions, music, video details, engagement rate and author stats (followers, following, total likes, verified). A summary mode returns one row per creator, and a monitor mode returns only posts that are new since the last run.

Limits, stated plainly

Public profiles only, no login. Run time is about the same as the leader: median 14.1 s against 14.7 s over 9 same-input runs each, so we do not claim it is faster. Private accounts and anything TikTok hides from logged-out visitors are not returned.
