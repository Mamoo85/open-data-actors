UK Sold Prices + EPC Rating API: Land Registry Data

How it works

Look up UK sold house prices from HM Land Registry and get the official EPC energy rating, score, floor area and price per m2 for each sale, matched by address. Search by postcode, town, district or county. Median stats and a new-sales monitor included. Official open data, no login. Beta.

Input options
