Bluesky Scraper API: Posts, Profiles, Followers

How it works

Scrape Bluesky posts by keyword or hashtag, author feeds, profiles, followers and follows from the public API. Engagement counts, links, media and dates. No login.

Input options

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
