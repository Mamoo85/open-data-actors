YouTube sponsorship tracker: sponsors, promo codes and tracked links

Who it is for: Brands and agencies researching who sponsors which creators, creator managers, and affiliate teams.

The problem

Sponsor reads sit inside videos and descriptions. Finding which brands paid which channels, with promo codes and links, means watching and reading each video.

How it works

Give channels, videos or brand names. Each run returns one row per detected sponsorship: sponsor brand and domain, promo code, tracked link, timestamp in the video, disclosure flags, a confidence level and the evidence text.
