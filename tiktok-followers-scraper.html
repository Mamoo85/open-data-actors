TikTok followers scraper: follower and following lists with profile stats

Who it is for: Influencer and brand teams studying an account's audience, researchers, community managers and AI agents.

The problem

TikTok does not export follower lists and loads them a few at a time. Seeing who follows an account, and how big those accounts are, is impossible by hand at scale.

How it works

Give public usernames or profile URLs and how many followers or following to read. Each run returns one row per person with handle, follower, following, video and like counts, and verified and private flags.

Limits, stated plainly

Public accounts only. TikTok returns follower lists in pages and empties some, so we retry with a residential session, which is why a run takes about two minutes. Handles are masked in the sample here because these are private individuals; the actor returns them.
