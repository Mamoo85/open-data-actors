Google Search Results Scraper: Real URLs, Sitelinks, PAA

How it works

Track what Google ranks for any query and country: organic results with real target URLs, titles, descriptions, sitelinks, ads, People Also Ask and related searches. Language, site: and date filters.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
