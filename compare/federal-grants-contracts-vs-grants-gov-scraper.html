What the publisher says it does

Ours: Open and forecasted federal grant opportunities from Grants.gov plus contract and grant awards from USAspending.gov, in one unified schema: deadlines, ceilings, amounts, agency, NAICS/PSC, recipient, and a link to the official record. No API key. $0.002 per opportunity, $0.0015 per award.

solidcode: [💰 $8.00 / 1K] Extract US federal grant opportunities from Grants.gov — titles, agencies, deadlines, award amounts, eligibility, and contacts. Filter by keyword, agency, category, applicant type, status, and posted date.

How to choose

  • Grants.gov Federal Grants Scraper has more users on the Store today (8 against our 2), which is a fair reason to look at it first.
  • Run each one on the same small input and compare the datasets you get back. That is the only reliable test of output quality, and it costs cents.
  • Our output columns, types and a real sample are on the schema page. For Grants.gov Federal Grants Scraper, use the sample and output tab on its own listing.
