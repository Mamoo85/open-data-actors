What the publisher says it does

Ours: Local business leads from OpenStreetMap by city, county, bounding box or radius: name, category, address, phone, website, business email, opening hours and socials, with a no-website flag, completeness score and last-edit date. Keyless, new-only monitor mode.

compass: Extract data from thousands of Google Maps locations and businesses, including reviews, reviewer details, images, contact info, including full name, email, and job title, opening hours, prices & more. Export data, run via API, schedule and monitor runs, or integrate with other tools.

How to choose

  • Google Maps Scraper has more users on the Store today (681831 against our 2), which is a fair reason to look at it first.
  • Run each one on the same small input and compare the datasets you get back. That is the only reliable test of output quality, and it costs cents.
  • Our output fields are listed on the OpenStreetMap Business Leads page. For Google Maps Scraper, use the sample and output tab on its own listing.
