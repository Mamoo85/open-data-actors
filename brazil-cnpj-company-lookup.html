Brazil CNPJ Lookup API: Company, Partners, CNAE

How it works

Look up Brazilian companies by CNPJ list: legal name, status, CNAE activities, partners (QSA), address, phone, capital and tax regime from Receita Federal open data. No login.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
cnpjsCNPJ numbersnoBrazilian company IDs (CNPJ), one per line, with or without dots, slash and dash (33.000.167/0001-01 or 33000167000101). You can paste a whole column from a spreadsheet. Check...
maxItemsMax companiesnoStop after this many company records. You are charged per company record returned.
checkBranchesUpToAlso check branch numbers up tonoFor each CNPJ, also look up its branches (filiais) numbered 0001 to this number, using the company root. 0 = off. Branches that do not exist are skipped silently and never charged.
onlyActiveOnly active companiesnoReturn only companies whose registration status is ATIVA. Others are skipped and not charged.
statesOnly these states (UF)noTwo-letter state codes such as SP, RJ, MG. Companies elsewhere are skipped and not charged. Useful together with branch checks.
includePartnersInclude partners (QSA)noInclude the partner and administrator list with role, entry date and age range. Personal tax IDs are masked by the Receita Federal.
includeSecondaryActivitiesInclude secondary CNAE activitiesnoInclude the full list of secondary economic activities.
reportUnresolvedAdd a free row for CNPJs that could not be resolvednoAdds a row with lookupStatus not_found, invalid or error so you can reconcile your list. These rows are never charged.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
