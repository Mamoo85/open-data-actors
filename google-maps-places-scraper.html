Google Maps scraper: places, ratings and details as a table

Who it is for: Local-lead and agency teams, web designers, sales ops, market researchers and AI agents that need Google Maps business data in a spreadsheet.

The problem

Google Maps shows one business at a time. Building a list of every plumber in Tampa, with ratings, review counts and whether it has a website, means copying hundreds of listings by hand.

How it works

Give a search term and a city (or a Maps URL). Each run returns one row per place: title, categories, address parts, rating, review count, opening hours, website and domain, coordinates, place ID, description, owner-claimed flag and permanently or temporarily closed flags. Closed places are skipped by default. Filters for website, phone and rating; optional contact details and reviews.

Limits, stated plainly

Reviews are capped at 5 per place. Service-area businesses have no street address on Maps, so address fields are empty for them. With details on, runs took 16-28 s against 11-22 s for the leader in our test, so speed is about equal, not better. Public pages only; phone and email columns are removed from the free sample.
