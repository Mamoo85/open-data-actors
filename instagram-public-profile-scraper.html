Instagram public profile scraper: followers, posts and engagement without login

Who it is for: Influencer and brand teams vetting accounts, agencies tracking competitors, lead generation, social analysts and AI agents.

The problem

Instagram shows one profile at a time and now asks for a login after a few views. Comparing 50 creators or competitors by followers and engagement means opening each by hand.

How it works

Give public usernames or profile URLs. Each run returns one row per profile with counts, bio, links, business fields and engagement figures computed from the latest posts (average and median likes, comments, engagement rate).

Limits, stated plainly

Public profiles only. Private accounts return the public fields only. Business fields are unavailable logged-out for brands where Instagram errors.
