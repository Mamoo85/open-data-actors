Facebook and Instagram ads from the Meta Ad Library

Who it is for: Marketers and agencies researching competitor creative, ecommerce and app teams, ad-intelligence tools, journalists.

The problem

The Meta Ad Library is public, but it has no simple export of ads by keyword or advertiser.

How it works

Give search terms, advertiser Page ids or pasted Ad Library URLs, plus country, active status and media filters; get one flat row per ad with advertiser, ad text, headline, link, call to action, image and video URLs, dates, platforms and variation count.

Limits, stated plainly

Returns only what the Ad Library publishes: no targeting, no exact spend for commercial ads and no ad performance. Spend and reach ranges appear only on political and issue ads. Meta rate-limits datacenter IPs, so residential proxy is the default. Image and video URLs are Meta CDN links that expire after some days.

Price

Pay per result on Apify, no subscription: $0.00065 per ad plus $0.00005 per run start. Duplicates and, in new-ads-only mode, ads already delivered are not charged. The live price is on the actor page.
