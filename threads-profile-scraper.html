Threads profile scraper: posts, views and likes without login

Who it is for: Brand and social teams tracking accounts, analysts comparing reach, and AI agents.

The problem

Threads gives no export of a profile. Comparing views, likes and replies across accounts or over a date range means copying posts one at a time.

How it works

Give public Threads usernames or profile URLs. Each run returns the profile (followers, bio, verified flag) and posts with text, views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, media type, hashtags, links and exact date. Filter by date.
