Federal Register Rules and Notices Monitor: Regulatory API

How it works

Federal Register monitor for new federal rules, proposed rules and regulatory notices from the free Federal Register API: agencies, effective and comment dates, abstract, CFR references, significance and an impact score. No login, no proxy, no personal data.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
documentTypesDocument typesnoWhich Federal Register document types to read. Leave empty for final rules and proposed rules.
daysBackPublication window (days)noKeep documents published in the last N days.
searchTermSearch termnoOptional full-text search, for example "cybersecurity" or "emissions".
agenciesAgency slugsnoOptional agency slugs from federalregister.gov/agencies, for example environmental-protection-agency or food-and-drug-administration.
significantOnlySignificant documents onlynoTurn on to keep only documents the agency marks as significant.
openCommentPeriodOnlyOpen comment period onlynoTurn on to keep only documents whose public comment period has not closed yet.
minImpactScoreMinimum impact scorenoOptional 0 to 100. Keep only rows at or above this score.
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many rows, newest first. You pay only for rows delivered.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
