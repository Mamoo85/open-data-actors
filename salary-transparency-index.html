Salary Benchmark Data: Pay Ranges by Role From Job Posts

How it works

Get pay-range percentiles (p10-p90) by role, seniority and location from pay-transparent job posts on public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards, with sample size, confidence and top payers.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
