UK House Sold Prices API: Land Registry Data, Pay per Result

How it works

Search UK house sold prices from HM Land Registry Price Paid Data by postcode, street, town or county, with date, property type and new-build filters, median/average prices and a new-sales monitor. Beta.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
postcodesPostcodesnoFull UK postcodes, e.g. M1 1AE. One search per postcode. If given, towns, districts and counties are ignored.
townsTownsnoTown names as recorded by HM Land Registry, e.g. LEEDS. Exact match.
districtsDistrictsnoLocal authority district names, e.g. CITY OF WESTMINSTER. Exact match.
countiesCountiesnoCounty names, e.g. WEST YORKSHIRE or GREATER LONDON. Exact match.
streetStreet (optional)noNarrow the area to one street name, e.g. NEWTON STREET. Exact match, combined with the town, district, county or postcode.
yearsBackYears backnoOnly sales in the last N years. Ignored if Date from is set.
dateFromDate from (YYYY-MM-DD)noEarliest transfer date, e.g. 2024-01-01.
dateToDate to (YYYY-MM-DD)noLatest transfer date.
propertyTypesProperty typesnoLeave empty for all types.
newBuildNew buildsnoFilter by the new-build flag.
minPriceMin price (GBP)noOptional lower price bound.
maxPriceMax price (GBP)noOptional upper price bound.
maxItemsMax itemsnoCap on sale records delivered (and charged) this run, split evenly across the places searched. Most recent first.
includeStatsAdd sold-price statisticsnoAdds one free summary row per place: count, median, average, min, max and a breakdown by property type.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
