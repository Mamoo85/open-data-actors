IRS Nonprofit 990 Financials and Health Score API

How it works

Search IRS tax-exempt organizations and get Form 990 financials: revenue, expenses, net assets, officer compensation total and a financial-health score. Uses the free ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API. No login.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
querySearch keywordsnoWords to find in organization names, for example food, youth, hospital or animal rescue. Leave empty to browse by state and category only.
stateStatenoTwo-letter US state code, for example MI, CA or NY. Leave empty for all states.
nteeCategoryCategorynoBroad NTEE category of the organization.
einsEINs (optional)noLook up specific organizations by 9-digit EIN (with or without the dash). When filled, the search fields above are ignored.
includeFinancialsAdd latest 990 financialsnoFetch each organization's latest Form 990 numbers and compute the health score. Turn off for a fast list of names only.
maxItemsMax organizationsnoStop after this many organizations. You pay only for rows delivered.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
