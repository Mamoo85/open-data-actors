Company Leadership Finder: Founders and Executives

How it works

Give it company domains, get the founders, executives and managers a company lists on its own team, about and leadership pages: name, title, seniority, source page. No LinkedIn, no login, no email guessing.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
domainsCompany domainsnoCompany websites to read, one per line: domains (posthog.com) or full URLs. Only the company's own public team, about, leadership and press pages are fetched, honouring robots.txt.
minSeniorityMinimum senioritynoDrop people below this level. 'any' keeps everyone with a job title.
maxPeoplePerDomainMax people per domainnoCap on people returned for each domain (most senior first). A domain is charged once no matter how many people it returns.
maxPagesPerDomainMax pages per domainnoHow many pages to read on each site (home page plus team, about, leadership and press pages).
maxDomainsMax domainsnoStop after this many domains from the list.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
