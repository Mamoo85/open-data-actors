FMCSA Authority Suspension Alerts for Carriers

How it works

Find trucking carriers that just received an FMCSA operating authority suspension notice. Each row has days until it takes effect, a 0-100 risk score, phone, email, address, power units and a SAFER link. Public federal data, no login.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
daysBackServed within last (days)noKeep notices FMCSA served in the last N days (1 to 120). Default 14.
includeVoluntaryInclude voluntary suspensionsnoOff by default: only involuntary notices (FMCSA-initiated, usually 30 days before authority is suspended). Turn on to add voluntary suspensions requested by the carrier.
actionTypesAction types (optional)noOptional override. Allowed values: involuntary-suspension, involuntary-revocation, voluntary-suspension, revocation, reinstatement. When empty, the include-voluntary switch...
statesCarrier states (optional)noOptional two-letter US state codes of the carrier physical address, for example TX, CA. Empty means all states.
minPowerUnitsMinimum power unitsnoKeep carriers with at least this many power units (trucks and tractors) on their census filing. 0 keeps everyone, including carriers with no census match.
minRiskScoreMinimum risk scorenoOptional 0 to 100. Keep only rows at or above this risk score.
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many rows, highest risk score first. You pay only for rows delivered.
