Facebook pages scraper: followers, category and ad status without login

Who it is for: Sales and agency teams researching businesses, brand monitors, market researchers and AI agents.

The problem

Facebook pages hide contact details, follower counts and ad status behind a login wall and scrolling. Checking 100 business pages by hand is slow.

How it works

Give public Facebook Page URLs. Each run returns one row per page: name, category, followers, website, social links, rating, price range, hours, page ID, creation date and whether the page is running ads. The sample CSV leaves out contact columns.
