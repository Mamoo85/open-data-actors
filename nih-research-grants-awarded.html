NIH Research Grants Awarded Tracker: NIH Grants Scraper

How it works

NIH research grants awarded tracker: list NIH awards by year, state, institute or topic from the free public NIH RePORTER API with project number, organization, award amount and date, activity code, abstract snippet and a funding score. No login.

Input options

FieldNameRequiredWhat it does
fiscalYearFiscal yearnoNIH fiscal year of the awards (October to September). 2026 is the most recent full year with awards.
minAwardAmountMinimum award amount (USD)noOnly grants with at least this award amount. Use 0 to include every funded grant.
statesOrganization statesnoTwo-letter US state codes of the awarded organization, for example MI or CA. Leave empty for all states.
agenciesInstitutes or centersnoNIH institute or center abbreviations, for example NCI, NIAID or NHLBI. Leave empty for all.
searchTextSearch wordsnoOnly grants whose title or abstract contains all these words, for example diabetes or machine learning.
maxItemsMax rowsnoStop after this many rows, newest award first. You pay only for rows delivered.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
