Hiring Signal Bundle: Open Roles, Trend & Salary per Company

How it works

Hiring signal bundle that returns one row per company: open roles, new roles in the last 7 and 30 days, hiring trend, department and seniority mix, top roles with links, published salary ranges and a 0-100 hiring score. Covers 8 job sources in one run. No login.

Limits, stated plainly

Public data only, no login. Fields a source does not publish come back empty rather than guessed. Coverage and freshness follow the underlying public source.
