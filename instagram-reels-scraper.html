Instagram reels scraper: plays, comments and music

Who it is for: Creator and brand teams studying what reels perform, agencies, and AI agents.

The problem

Reels stats are visible only one reel at a time and behind a login after a few views. Comparing plays and comments across an account means opening every reel.

How it works

Give public profile or reel URLs. Each run returns one row per reel: caption, hashtags, mentions, plays, likes, comment counts, duration, music details, video URL and timestamp.
